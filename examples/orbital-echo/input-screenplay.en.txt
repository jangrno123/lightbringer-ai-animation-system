INT. LIGHTBRINGER OBSERVATION ROOM - ARTIFICIAL NIGHT

Blue starlight cuts across the cracked observation window. Alarm beacons rotate slowly. Pilot ARIA rests one hand on a fractured holographic console.

ARIA
(steadying her breath, eyes fixed on the window)
Forty-two seconds until the jump gate closes. If we miss it now, there is no way back.

The route on the console breaks into red fragments. Navigator NOX appears as a cyan hologram.

NOX (O.S.)
(low and controlled)
Unregistered life signs detected along the starboard hull.

The ceiling panel bends inward. The black reconnaissance drone WARDEN-7 descends, unfolding six legs. Metal strain echoes through the observation room.

ARIA
(turning toward the drone)
Drop the emergency bulkhead. I will verify the signal.

Nox's hologram flickers. The blue light of the closing jump gate reflects in the drone's single optic.
