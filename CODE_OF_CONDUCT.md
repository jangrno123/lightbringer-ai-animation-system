# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

We want production workers from creative and technical disciplines to discuss AI animation without harassment, dismissal, or pressure to disclose confidential work.

Positive participation includes:

- respecting artistic, production, engineering, and research expertise;
- separating measured results from speculation;
- giving actionable critique;
- acknowledging labor, consent, rights, and production constraints;
- protecting confidential and unreleased material;
- welcoming people with different levels of technical experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- publishing private information or production material without permission;
- deliberately misleading performance or cost claims;
- repeated promotional spam;
- using generated content to impersonate contributors or rights holders.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through STUDIO GENESIS's official contact channel at [studiogenesis.co.kr](https://www.studiogenesis.co.kr). Do not include sensitive material in a public issue.
